Task-checking Skill (operator-authored, source/development probe)

Before deciding, identify the attribute the current question asks about: abnormality, imaging modality, organ or body location, or image plane. Judge that attribute; a visible disease does not decide an unrelated modality, location or plane question.

For an abnormality question, inspect both lung fields and the cardiac silhouette in the original image. If a particular finding or region is named, judge that finding in that region rather than substituting a generic abnormality. Check the question's polarity: evidence for an abnormal lung is not evidence that the lung is healthy.

Use only what the original image supports. Source-example diagnoses and answers do not transfer to the current patient. Do not infer a previous examination or history from a single image.
