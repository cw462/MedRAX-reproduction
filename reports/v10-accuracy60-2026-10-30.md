## Comparison

P0 uses the existing eight-tool agent at temperature 0.2, top_p 0.95, requested
output cap 1024, 240 seconds, 12 tool calls and 16 model calls. The three candidate
arms share one option-difference plan made from current questions and original
images, without P0's final answer or K/E. The plan occupies one of each arm's four
model slots but is billed once. Each arm has at most three additional model calls,
two tool attempts including real pixel crops, and one disagreement gate.

- **V:** independent option-directed visual reinspection, with full images retained
  when inspecting crops or comparing views.
- **VK:** V plus at most two applicable discriminative K rules before choosing actions.
- **VKE:** VK plus at most one positive and one negative B action experience.

The existing 59-card BM25 + MiniLM dense + RRF knowledge index is retained. B action
cards derive from the existing sixty training trajectories: 25 correct, 20 incorrect,
15 execution errors. Their structured records do not contain clinical observation
prose, so cards explicitly retain whole-question weak supervision and unknown step
causality. Missing relevant K/E is an abstention, not forced injection.

The V9.1 bundle referenced by the plan was found in cloud-task history but was not
available in the local/server filesystem. Necessary crop, image-return and tolerant
candidate modules were implemented in this repository; this is not a literal merge
of that unacquired bundle. The original V9 implementation and results are retained.

## Execution and accounting

The first two fixed inputs run all three arms. An automatic label-free check requires
legal P0/candidate answers and actual image payloads before continuing. Then all
remaining P0/VKE pairs run, followed by V/VK ablations using the same saved P0 and
planning records. Ordinary malformed optional plan fields do not abort a candidate;
uncertainty does not suppress a legal best answer. A prior independent legal answer
is retained when a later formatting request fails, with the failure recorded.

The workflow reuses the existing `api_budget_qwen_100cny_v9.json` ledger, identity,
inheritance and cap unchanged. The initial committed value was CNY 139.201948;
these are existing assumed-rate accounting values, not provider invoices. Each API
request still reserves against the ledger. No budget increase or fresh spending pool
was created for V10.

Generation writes private checkpoints under `runs/v10_accuracy60`. A separate scorer
opens isolated labels only after 60 P0, 60 shared plans and 180 branch records reach
complete generation. Overall keeps the denominator 60; seven overlapping category
results are also reported. P0-failure recovery is separated from correction of a
wrong legal P0 answer. The primary net gain is corrected minus harmed cases.
