## Question and fixed comparison

The preceding fixed sixty-question development experiment scored P0/S0/S1/S2 at
39/40/38/41 out of 60. S2 did not reach the minimum target of 42/60. Its five
corrections of legal P0 answers were offset by five harmful changes; the total
gain over P0 came from two inherited technical-failure recoveries. The prespecified
S3 change was to place an independent image reader before any exposure to the
competing options, then match its observations to the same frozen pair.

S3 reuses the original V10 P0 answers and VKE candidates, original images, and
the existing S1 anonymous comparison packets. The sixty questions and candidate
answers are not resampled, regenerated, or rewritten. Exactly **21 questions**
with two legal but different answers receive new inference. The **37 agreements**
inherit their common answer; **two missing P0 answers** inherit the existing
independent candidate. This routing depends only on answer equality and presence,
never correctness, the S1/S2 outcome lists, or the diagnostic candidate union.

## Two separated model requests

Both the reader and matcher use the **same `qwen3-vl-plus` model**. "Independent"
here means separate input and conversation contexts: the reader does not see
the candidate options before producing its observation. It does **not** mean
different model weights, independently trained systems, or statistical independence.
Their errors may remain correlated; this experiment does not identify the cause
of any remaining error.

1. **Answer-blind image reader.** It receives only the current question stem and
   full original images. It receives no option texts or letters, P0 answer,
   independent candidate, prior selector choice, candidate-derived follow-up
   question, cached ROI, tool diagnostic output, knowledge card, or experience
   advice. It records concise observable findings with image references and
   unresolved limits. The output is a fallible visual report, not a reference
   diagnosis or a clinically verified explanation.
2. **Anonymous matcher.** It receives the unchanged S1 X/Y comparison packet,
   including the same full option texts, original/cached images, and neutralized
   raw tool evidence, plus the new independent observation. It uses the same
   source-anonymous mapping and symmetric choice requirements. Neither answer
   receives priority merely because the evidence remains incomplete. It chooses
   the current best legal X/Y without a new tool action or another reading round.

The reader's input boundary is deliberately narrower than the matcher's. The
matcher retains S1 evidence so this run does not also remove its earlier evidence
context. Nevertheless, **S3 is not a single-factor comparison under equal request
budgets**: relative to S1, each disagreement gets one extra image-reading call,
and the matcher gets that call's observation. Any gain would apply to this combined
procedure and extra budget, not prove that anonymity or sequencing alone caused it.

## Fixed limits and failure handling

There are at most **two new model requests per disagreement**, hence at most
**42 new requests** over the fixed sixty; no new crop, tool acquisition, repair
request, or iterative debate is planned. The existing V11 model, image settings,
fixed X/Y seed, requested output cap, and provider profile remain frozen. The
original ledger and its actual current policy cap are reused without a new budget
or limit increase. No claim is made that the original V10 calls are free provider
history; they are simply cached and are not invoked again by S3.

A missing or failed independent observation is recorded explicitly rather than
invented or silently paid for again. An API or choice failure follows the frozen
technical fallback to anonymous X, with no systematic preference for P0. Such a
fallback is separated from successful model selection in the report. Completed
terminals are reused on recovery, while an indeterminate started request is not
automatically repeated. V10, S1, and S2 saved records remain unchanged.

Generation is isolated under **`runs/v11_blindread60`**, with server code in
**`code_v11_blindread`**. Only this new output directory receives S3 checkpoints,
results, accounting and reports. The reader may not consume scored reports or
reference labels; matcher routing is fixed before isolated scoring.

## Evaluation and reporting

The independent scorer opens labels only after all sixty terminal records and
the completed generation status exist. `v11_blindread_metrics.py` wraps the
unchanged V11 scoring arithmetic for the S3 stage name; it does not rewrite the
older scorer or any S1/S2 outputs. The optional `--previous-run` points to S1.
S2 comparisons are taken from its already completed aggregate report.

Report P0/S0/S1/S2/S3 correct counts over 60 and accuracy, keeping correction of
wrong legal P0 answers, P0-failure recovery, and harm separate. Also report how
many of the eight corrective and eight harmful frozen candidates were preserved
or blocked, distinguishing technical fallbacks; seven overlapping task categories;
new model calls, elapsed time and recorded ledger costs; and representative actual
answer transitions. Case examples may show identifiers, answer letters and scored
outcomes, but must not reconstruct unseen clinical causes from correctness.

The first development target remains **42/60**, followed by **45/60**; the fixed
development set does not establish independent main-table performance. The
label-derived union upper bound is only a retrospective diagnostic and is never
an achieved algorithm result or a selector input. A K/E-specific contribution
would require a subsequent matched comparison with V/VK/VKE under the same frozen
selection procedure. Experience-card upgrading and B/C comparison remain deferred
until the selection route warrants them.

## Next decision after S3

S3 did not improve on S2 and did not reach 42/60. The extra reader call is not an
equal-budget causal test of input separation alone, and no root cause has been
established from these outcomes. No new experiment, experience-card upgrade, or
B/C comparison is started automatically. A possible next step is to examine the
recorded harmful changes before specifying a separate controlled experiment; a
different adjudicator model would require an explicit matched comparison and its
own budget. These are proposals, not completed analyses or launched runs.
