Status: **S1 and S2 completed and independently scored**, with the final workflow

## Frozen comparison

This experiment starts from the V10 implementation and report at
`48b0da61ee76791592f4f2b47592b6120c36c950`; the V11 implementation is saved in local
commit `7d4df54`. It reuses all original P0 answers, VKE
candidates, original images, cached local images, and raw tool observations. No
V10 prediction or acquisition is rerun or overwritten. The overall denominator
remains the original fixed 60 development questions. This is a new selection
experiment on a previously examined development set, not an independent test set.

- **S0:** the saved V10 VKE gate result, 40/60; no new calls.
- **S1:** source-anonymous, symmetric X/Y comparison. Exactly 21 legal-answer
  disagreements receive one new model request each. The 37 agreements inherit
  the common answer; the two missing P0 answers inherit the legal independent
  candidate. These routes depend only on saved answer equality and availability.
- **S2:** if S1 explicitly records `needs_followup=true` with a nonempty standalone
  visual question, obtain at most one targeted independent observation and select
  again. All other questions retain S1. Triggering never uses reference labels,
  correctness, a curated error list, or which candidate was previously rejected.

X/Y order is assigned by the fixed seed `medrax-v11-symmetric-20261030` and question
ID, without consulting correctness. Complete option texts are shown with the
original option letters and source identities hidden. The original question stem
is preserved. Evidence receives neutral image/observation IDs and a fixed shuffled
record order; private paths and acquisition-source markers are removed from the
model-facing text. Both choices receive the same originals, cached pixels and raw
tool returns. Neither candidate defenses nor the previous gate output is included.

The selector compares distinguishing properties with the same standards. Shared
uncertainty gives neither answer priority. It must choose the best legal X/Y and
can separately identify a remaining visual question. Noncritical JSON metadata
errors do not erase an unambiguous legal choice. Actual API/choice failure falls
back to the fixed anonymous X, not systematically to P0; this is recorded as a
technical fallback, never a successful decision.

## S2 action and reader separation

The S2 action planner receives the standalone visual question, cached images, and
the already retrieved K/E action advice. It receives no competing answer texts or
S1 choice. It chooses one reinspection of existing images or one crop from one
original. All full originals remain available. The independent reader receives
the visual question and selected pixels, without answer choices, S1 decision,
historical action advice, or candidate defenses. Its observation, explicitly
fallible and bound to image references, then goes to the same anonymous comparator.

There are at most three additional model calls per triggered question: action
planning, independent reading, and symmetric reselection; at most one crop/tool
attempt and one independent observation. No debate loop or second observation is
allowed. Invalid source-dependent follow-up questions are skipped with a recorded
error. A technical S2 failure retains the saved S1 answer. S2 records only its
incremental resources and does not charge inherited S1 computations again.

S1 and S2 use the same `qwen3-vl-plus`, temperature 0.2, top_p 0.95, requested output
cap 1024, image detail `auto`, and no SDK retries. Each request has a 120-second
timeout; S1 has a 240-second case guard and S2 has 480 seconds for its maximum three
sequential requests. The original `ProviderLedger` is retained, with the actual
existing policy cap read at runtime. No new ledger, budget increase or literal
copied cap is used. An indeterminate interrupted request is not automatically paid
again; terminal results are resumed without rewriting them.

## Image handling and evaluation

Cached local images of width or height at most 10 pixels are expanded to at least
32 pixels per dimension from the same original when its dimensions permit; if the
parent binding is unavailable, the invalid local is omitted and the full image
retained. No interpolation or invented detail is used. V10 cached files are not
modified. S2 follows the same minimum-size rule for any new crop.

Each generation process opens only inputs and saved predictions/observations.
The separate scorer requires a complete sixty-question stage before opening
reference labels. It reports P0/S0/S1/S2 correct counts, correction of legal wrong
answers, P0 failure recovery, harms, retention of the eight correct VKE candidates,
blocking of the eight harmful candidates, seven overlapping task categories,
representative answer transitions, added calls, time, and assumed-rate costs.

The development goals remain 42/60 (+5 percentage points over P0), 45/60 (+10
points), and 48/60 (80% absolute). Only a future comparison under the same frozen
selector can attribute final-answer improvements specifically to K/E. Experience
card reconstruction from training observations and B/C comparison are deferred
until the selection route shows useful results.

## Completed S1 results

The original fixed sixty-question denominator is retained. S1 made one new
comparison for each of 21 legal-answer disagreements, inherited 37 agreements,
and recovered the two missing P0 answers using the existing independent candidate.
All 60 terminal records completed; no selector error or technical fallback occurred.

| Arm | Correct / 60 | Accuracy | Wrong legal P0 answers corrected | P0 failures recovered | Correct P0 answers harmed | Total net vs P0 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P0 | 39/60 | 65.0% | — | — | — | — |
| S0: saved V10 VKE gate | 40/60 | 66.7% | 1 | 2 | 2 | +1 |
| S1: anonymous symmetric selection | 38/60 | 63.3% | 3 | 2 | 6 | -1 |

S1 retained **3 of the 8 corrective candidates** and blocked **2 of the 8 harmful
candidates**. All three preserved corrections and both blocked harms came from
normal selection; none came from technical fallback. Five available corrections
were still rejected, while six harmful candidates were selected. Relative to S0,
two extra corrections were outweighed by four extra harms, producing **two fewer
correct answers**. Relative to P0, **3 corrections + 2 failure recoveries - 6 harms
= -1**. Excluding the two failure recoveries, the net change is **-3**; among the
58 questions with a legal P0 answer, S1 scored 36/58 against P0's 39/58.

These results do not support an accuracy gain from anonymous comparison alone.
The 42/60 first development target was not met. The previously discussed
label-derived candidate union is only a diagnostic upper bound, not an achieved
algorithm result, an inference-time route, or evidence of a K/E contribution.

## Completed S2 results

S2 ran from **23:42:33.967 to 23:49:02.039 Beijing time on 2026-10-30**, using
exactly **19** S1 records with a legal normal selector result,
`needs_followup=true`, and a nonempty standalone visual question. Its triggers
were taken from the frozen S1 outputs, not from scored correction or harm lists.
Each triggered question received one independent observation and three model
calls; the other 41 questions retained S1 without new calls. The recorded actions
were **9 original-image reinspections, 9 original-pixel crop attempts, and 1
existing-local image inspection**. The crop count records attempts, not a verified
count of successful or clinically useful new image returns. No selector error or
technical fallback occurred. All sixty terminal records completed before the
isolated S2 scorer read labels.

| Arm | Correct / 60 | Accuracy | Wrong legal P0 answers corrected | P0 failures recovered | Correct P0 answers harmed | Total net vs P0 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P0 | 39/60 | 65.0% | — | — | — | — |
| S0: saved V10 VKE gate | 40/60 | 66.7% | 1 | 2 | 2 | +1 |
| S1: anonymous symmetric selection | 38/60 | 63.3% | 3 | 2 | 6 | -1 |
| S2: one targeted observation | 41/60 | 68.3% | 5 | 2 | 5 | +2 |

S2 preserved **5/8 corrective candidates** and blocked **3/8 harmful candidates**,
all through normal selections. Three available corrections were still rejected,
and five harmful candidates were selected. Compared with S1, seven answers
changed: **five became correct and two became incorrect**, yielding **+3 questions**.
S2 is **+1 question versus S0** and **+2 versus P0**. However, **5 corrections - 5
harms = 0** on legal P0 answers: S2 and P0 both scored **39/58** on that subset.
The overall +2 is accounted for by the two inherited P0-failure recoveries and
does not establish net medical-reasoning improvement among valid P0 responses.

The S2 score also equals the existing **41/60 raw VKE candidate** score; accepting
every candidate was a cached reference comparison and required no new run.
The 42/60 minimum development target was **not met**, and the diagnostic
label-derived union upper bound is not an achieved score. This VKE-only selector
comparison cannot establish a K/E-specific final-answer benefit.

## Next decision

S1 did not improve accuracy. S2 improved upon S1, but its legal-P0 correction/harm
balance remains zero and its final score does not exceed the cached raw candidate
score. Experience-card upgrading and B/C representation comparison therefore
remain deferred. The **single proposed next change** is to move the independent
image reader before exposure to either candidate, then match its observations to
the competing options. This is a recommendation for a separately specified next
experiment, not a run started here. No new paid experiment, data selection, or
inference change was made after scoring these results.
