## What this round says

* The data exclude a **B gain over A larger than +12 accuracy points**; the point estimate is -1.7.
* The data exclude a **C gain over B larger than +7 accuracy points**; the point estimate is -6.8.
* No arm separates from the no-memory baseline, and the two representations cost 0.119–0.134 CNY per question without buying accuracy in this round.

Per the rule agreed before the run (if C is not clearly better than B, redesign the representation and injection instead of adding arms), rounds 2–3 stay cancelled and the next step is a redesign, not more repeats of this one.

## What this round cannot settle

* **Power.** The 95% interval half-width here is about 14 accuracy points. A real difference smaller than that would be invisible at n=60; 'no detectable difference' is not 'no difference'.
* **Prompt length is not held equal.** In S0, C's block sat at the 1400-character cap while B's ran 554–1126 characters. Any B→C difference mixes representation with prompt length until the planned `B_length_control` ablation is run.
* **Repeats.** One round means model sampling noise is not averaged out; the same questions were not re-run.

## Pipeline integrity

Every terminal in these runs is a real tool-using episode: the scorer reads only the run's `results.jsonl` and the labels, and the ledger's per-call settlements are recorded in each terminal. The per-question letter matrix and the full type table are in `reports/s2_round1_table.md`.
